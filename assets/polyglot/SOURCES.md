# Polyglot workspace assets

All assets are served locally; the interface does not require a font or icon CDN.

- **Inter Variable**: official Inter project, https://github.com/rsms/inter. Font downloaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rsms/inter/master/docs/font-files/InterVariable.woff2. See `fonts/LICENSE.txt` for the SIL Open Font License.
- **Tabler outline icons, v3.34.1**: https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons. Distributed SVG files obtained from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@tabler/icons@3.34.1/icons/outline/. See `icons/LICENSE.txt` for the MIT license.
- **ST3GG mark and five sequence preview images**: cropped from the user's approved, generated eight-screen design review in `nested-polyglots-design-review-2026-10-21`. The mark comes from `01-configure-run.png`; sequence thumbnails come from `04-refine-images.png`.

Sequence thumbnails illustrate the preset only. They are not assigned as generated carrier bytes: a layer remains ungenerated until image generation, local carrier creation or an upload supplies its actual image.

The three ready-to-build examples deliberately reuse these bundled preview PNGs as actual carrier bytes. They are small demonstration images; normal AI sequence presets still use them only as previews.
